This collaboration developed a deep-learning pipeline to quantify bone marrow adiposity in UK Biobank Dixon MRI. The resulting imaging phenotypes were studied alongside genetic and clinical data in two 2025 *Nature Communications* papers.

Access to UK Biobank data is governed by UK Biobank rather than distributed by this website.
