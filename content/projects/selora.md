SeLoRA adapts a latent diffusion model for medical image synthesis using selectively allocated low-rank parameters. The work asks where adaptation capacity is most useful and how much of a large model needs to be trained for a specialised imaging task.

The public project page contains method details and examples. The paper was published in *Expert Systems with Applications* in 2026.
