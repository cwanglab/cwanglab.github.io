The review surveys what deep-learning measurement at biobank scale has changed in the study of bone marrow adiposity, drawing on the group's UK Biobank pipeline and the genome-wide and phenome-wide association studies it enabled. Written with Wei Xu and William P. Cawthorn of the University of Edinburgh.
