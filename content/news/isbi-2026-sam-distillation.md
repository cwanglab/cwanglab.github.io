The paper shows how active learning can direct knowledge distillation from a Segment Anything Model teacher, reducing the annotation budget needed to train compact medical segmentation models. The work was carried out with group members Yuchen Mao and Yuchen Pan alongside collaborators at Heriot-Watt and the University of Edinburgh, with Chengjia Wang as corresponding author.
